# A-B Sign! design

Use the supplied logo and its pastel colors. Green marks the next action and success, blue marks selection, and coral marks retry feedback. Slate and white frame lesson photos. Letter groups help learners scan the library.

Use Fredoka for headings and Varela Round for control labels. Show a large sign beside the choices in picture quizzes. Keep the check button, written feedback, and next action together.

Keep navigation labels consistent. Use a top bar for learning, camera, and the teacher builder. Play and Progress use a sidebar at wider desktop sizes. The builder has steps, editor, and learner preview panes, with quiz controls below. Use press feedback, short transitions, and a progress fill to show what changed. Respect reduced motion and keep written feedback available with sounds off.
